# Repository audit policy

Maintain exactly one repository audit document: `PUBLIC_RELEASE_AUDIT.md`.
Consolidate material outstanding release findings there. Do not add milestone
audit reports, engineering audit inventories/matrices, audit addenda, or separate
`PUBLIC_RELEASE_*` documents. Keep detailed development evidence in ignored
`build/` directories.

Normal README, deployment, Help, licensing/provenance, security and test
documentation remains valid. Preserve historical implementation evidence and
third-party notices; retirement of redundant audits must preserve useful findings
and their original evidence. Do not rewrite Git history.

The repository remains private. Source synchronization and private engineering
qualification do not authorize public releases, tags, artifact uploads or visibility
changes. Keep historical artwork/source-history review and applicable source/license
delivery gates open until separately resolved and owner approved.
